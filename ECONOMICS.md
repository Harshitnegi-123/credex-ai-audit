## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months

**What needs to be true:**

- 1,000 customers paying average $1,000/year = $1M ARR
- Requires ~8,000 consultations booked over 18 months
- Requires ~80,000 emails captured
- Requires ~320,000 audits completed
- Requires ~900,000 visitors over 18 months = ~50,000/month

**How to get there:**

Month 1–3: Organic only — 500 audits/month via Reddit, HN, Twitter
Month 4–6: Viral loop kicks in — shared URLs drive 30% of traffic
Month 7–12: Newsletter partnerships + Credex existing customer base
Month 13–18: SEO compounding + word of mouth

**Key assumption:** Viral coefficient of 0.3 — 
every 10 users share their audit link, 3 new users come in.
This is conservative given the shareable URL feature.
