# GTM — Go To Market Strategy

## Exact Target User

Engineering managers and CTOs at early-stage startups (5–50 employees) 
who are scaling their dev team and suddenly have 3–4 AI tool subscriptions 
running simultaneously. They signed up for tools during a trial, forgot to 
cancel, and are now paying for seats nobody uses.

## What They Google

- "chatgpt team vs plus worth it"
- "cursor vs github copilot for small team"
- "how much should we spend on AI tools"
- "reduce saas spend startup"

## Where They Hang Out Online

- r/startups, r/SideProject, r/ExperiencedDevs on Reddit
- Hacker News — Show HN posts
- IndieHackers.com community
- Twitter/X — following @levelsio, @marc_louvion, @swyx
- Slack groups — Online Geniuses, Demand Curve

## How to Get First 100 Users

**Day 1 — Hacker News:**
Post title: "Show HN: I built a free tool that audits 
your AI tool subscriptions in 60 seconds"
Target: Front page → 500+ visitors in 24 hours

**Day 2–3 — Reddit:**
r/startups post: "We were paying $800/month for ChatGPT 
Team — this tool showed us 3 people used it. 
Built a free audit tool so others don't make same mistake"
Personal story hook — not promotional

**Day 4–5 — Twitter thread:**
"I audited AI spend for 10 startup founders. 
Here's what I found: [thread]"
Tag relevant people: @levelsio @marc_louvion

**Unfair channel:**
Credex existing customer base — warm email:
"Before buying more credits, see if you're already 
overspending. Free audit: [link]"
Expected CTR: 15–20% vs cold traffic 2–3%

## Unfair Distribution Channel

Credex already has relationships with companies buying AI credits. 
Email existing customers with — "Free tool to check if you're 
overspending on AI before buying more credits." Warm audience, 
zero CAC.

## Week 1 Traction If This Works

- 200+ audits completed
- 40+ emails captured
- 15+ audit links shared on social media
- 3 inbound mentions from newsletters or communities
