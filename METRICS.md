# Metrics

## North Star Metric

**Audits completed per week**

Why: An audit completed means a user got real value — 
they saw their spend, got recommendations, and understood 
their savings opportunity. Everything else (emails, shares, 
conversions) flows from this. DAU is wrong for a tool people 
use quarterly. Revenue is too lagging at this stage.

## 3 Input Metrics That Drive the North Star

**1. Visitor to audit start rate**
- Measures: Is the landing page convincing enough?
- Target: >40% of visitors start filling the form
- If low: Fix headline, CTA, or add social proof

**2. Audit start to completion rate**
- Measures: Is the form too long or confusing?
- Target: >70% of started audits are completed
- If low: Simplify form, add progress indicator

**3. Shared audit URLs created per week**
- Measures: Is the viral loop working?
- Target: 1 share per 5 audits = 20% share rate
- If low: Make share button more prominent, 
  add incentive to share

## What to Instrument First

1. Audit form — how many users start vs complete
2. Share button — how many clicks per audit result
3. Email capture — conversion rate after results shown
4. Referral tracking — how many visitors came from shared URLs

## Pivot Trigger

If after 500 audits:
- Email capture rate is below 10%, or
- Share rate is below 5%, or
- Zero consultation bookings

Then pivot: Remove email gate entirely, 
focus on volume and SEO, monetize through 
Credex banner/CTA on results page instead.
