# User Interviews

## Interview 1

Name: Hemant Negi  
Role: Freelance Developer

Questions Asked:
1. Do you currently pay for AI tools?
2. Which tools do you use most?
3. What is frustrating about AI subscriptions?
4. Would cost-saving recommendations help?

Responses:
- Uses ChatGPT Plus and Cursor
- Pays monthly but does not track total spending carefully
- Mentioned that multiple subscriptions slowly become expensive
- Said he would like a simple dashboard showing unnecessary spending
- Did not care much about analytics graphs
- Preferred quick recommendations over detailed reports

Key Insight:
Users care more about simple savings suggestions than advanced dashboards.

## Interview 2

Name: Muskan Kanojia 
Role: MCA Student

Questions Asked:
1. Which AI tools do you use regularly?
2. Do you know how much you spend monthly?
3. Would you use an AI audit tool?

Responses:
- Mostly uses free versions of ChatGPT and Gemini
- Occasionally buys ChatGPT Plus during exams or project work
- Said subscription switching is confusing
- Felt that students usually do not compare pricing properly
- Liked the idea of receiving cheaper plan suggestions

Key Insight:
Many users are unaware of pricing differences between plans and often overpay temporarily during heavy usage periods.

## Interview 3

Name: Ajay Sahu
Role: DevOps Engineer

Questions Asked:
1. How many AI tools does your team currently use?
2. Is AI subscription spending monitored regularly?
3. What problems do you face with AI tool management?

Responses:
- Team mainly uses ChatGPT Team, Claude, and Cursor
- Mentioned that different team members often buy separate subscriptions
- Said small monthly AI expenses usually go unnoticed
- Noticed that inactive or rarely used seats still remain active for months
- Liked the idea of automated savings recommendations and shareable audit reports

Key Insight:
Teams using multiple AI tools often lose visibility into subscription waste because purchases are decentralized across developers and departments.
